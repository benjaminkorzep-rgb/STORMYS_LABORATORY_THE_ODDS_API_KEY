How to read a card. REPORTED = team-reported status via ESPN's feed. MARKET = DraftKings line via ESPN, converted to win chance with the bookmaker margin removed. INFERRED = this board's range: market and ESPN's model blended, widened ±5 points. Live games show ESPN's in-game win probability. Last-5 records are shown for context and labelled as noise.

Alerts fire on: a spread move of 2+ points from open, a total move of 3+, a moneyline win-chance move of 5+ points, a Questionable/Doubtful/Day-To-Day tag, and tip within 60 minutes (lineup lock). Small line wiggles and G League two-way listings do not alert.

Market context only, not betting advice. Starters are not shown as official until teams post them, about 30 min before tip.

Data: ESPN public scoreboard and game-summary feeds, read through your Firecrawl connector (about 1 credit per refresh; polling pauses while this tab is hidden).
